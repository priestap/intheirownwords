# Content and reuse

Original editorial summaries, introductions, responses, and context notes in this project are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribute “In Their Own Words contributors,” link to the source repository or site when available, and indicate changes.

Historical quotations are not original project content and are not relicensed by this project. The Calvin quotations use nineteenth-century English translations of sixteenth-century works; the Westminster Confession is a seventeenth-century text. Preserve the author, translation, precise reference, and source link when sharing. No modern introductions, source-host layouts, or editorial annotations from those hosts are reproduced.

Source hosts retain their own site material and marks. Linking to a source does not imply affiliation or endorsement. The software is separately licensed under the MIT License in LICENSE.
