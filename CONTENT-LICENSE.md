# Content and reuse

Original editorial summaries, introductions, responses, and context notes in this project are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribute “In Their Own Words contributors,” link to the source repository or site when available, and indicate changes.

Historical quotations are not original project content and are not relicensed by this project. The Calvin quotations use nineteenth-century English translations of sixteenth-century works; the Westminster Confession and Canons of Dort are seventeenth-century texts, and the Heidelberg Catechism and Belgic Confession are sixteenth-century texts. Added excerpts from the latter three follow the historic English texts published by the Free Reformed Churches of North America; source-host introductions and modern annotations are not reproduced. Preserve the author, translation, precise reference, and source link when sharing. No modern introductions, source-host layouts, or editorial annotations from those hosts are reproduced.

Source hosts retain their own site material and marks. Linking to a source does not imply affiliation or endorsement. The Second London Baptist Confession (1689) excerpts follow the English text hosted by Reformed Baptist Seminary and reproduce only the historic confession, not the host’s introductions or annotations.

The software is separately licensed under the MIT License in LICENSE.

The Second Helvetic Confession (1566) excerpts reproduce the historic English version in the appendix to Philip Schaff’s *The Creeds of Christendom*, Volume III, as transcribed by CCEL. This nineteenth-century English version is public domain; it is not a modern translation.

Modern quotations from cessationist writers and their critics remain copyrighted by their respective rights holders and are not offered under the project’s CC BY license. This collection uses brief, attributed excerpts for commentary and criticism, with links to the full sources. Preserve author, publication, reference, and source URL when citing them; further reuse requires your own assessment of the applicable rights.

Modern excerpts in **Christ’s Return & His People**, including criticisms, responses, supporting voices, and the creator’s separately linked personal confession, retain their respective copyrights. They are not relicensed under the editorial CC BY license. The historic Westminster, Heidelberg, and Belgic excerpts follow the source editions identified with each passage.

The Calvin collection also includes brief excerpts from modern critics, which retain their respective copyrights and are not relicensed under CC BY. The John Wesley and Friedrich Bente excerpts follow the historic editions identified in their citations; the source hosts’ modern introductions and annotations are not reproduced.

The millennium entry quotes Augustine’s *City of God* XX.9 in Marcus Dods’s historic translation (NPNF, First Series, vol. 2, 1887) and Calvin’s *Institutes* III.25.5 in Henry Beveridge’s translation (1845), as transcribed by CCEL. These historic passages are identified separately from modern copyrighted excerpts; later editorial annotations are not quoted as either author’s words.

Additional early Christian witness excerpts follow the NPNF translations as revised for New Advent, with the edition identified beside each passage. These are brief attributed quotations linked to their source pages; New Advent’s revised text and site material are not relicensed by this project.
